# ChatB2K — Paid Acquisition Launch Brief

**Brand:** Resonance Fitness  
**App:** ChatB2K  
**Canonical web companion:** https://chatb2k.resofit.fit  
**Primary market:** Nigeria  
**Campaign status:** Prepared as a launch framework only; no ads created, activated, or funded.

## Positioning

**Core promise:** One intelligent companion for wellness guidance, ResoFit discovery and Elite creator workflows.

**Proof points that may be used after production verification:**
- Secure account access through ResoFit.
- Chat with Coach B2K.
- Discover ResoFit programmes and products.
- Enter the Elite creator/media workspace.

Do not advertise native video generation, automatic Buffer publishing, native subscription purchase, offline chat history, or other capabilities that remain web-only or unverified in the current native app.

## Audience and creative tests

1. **Wellness seekers (Nigeria):** short-form video showing a person asking Coach B2K for a practical next step; CTA: “Meet ChatB2K”.
2. **Creators and streamers:** creator workflow and Elite workspace handoff; CTA: “Explore ChatB2K for creators”. Do not imply BIGO affiliation or guaranteed creator income.
3. **ResoFit customers:** discover a suitable programme/product and continue through the canonical ResoFit checkout; CTA: “Find your next ResoFit solution”.

## Draft ad copy

### Creative A — Coach B2K
**Hook:** “Your next step should be clear.”  
**Body:** “Meet ChatB2K by Resonance Fitness. Get practical guidance, discover ResoFit solutions and keep your next move organised.”  
**CTA:** Download ChatB2K.

### Creative B — One connected ecosystem
**Hook:** “Wellness. Discovery. Creator workflows.”  
**Body:** “Bring your ResoFit journey and creator workflow closer with ChatB2K.”  
**CTA:** Explore ChatB2K.

### Creative C — Nigerian audience
**Hook:** “No guesswork. Just your next move.”  
**Body:** “Chat with Coach B2K, explore ResoFit programmes and find the support that fits your goal.”  
**CTA:** Get the app.

All copy is subject to final feature, price, claims and store-link review before activation.

## Channel plan

- **TikTok Ads:** vertical 9:16 creator-led demos; test three hooks and two edits per concept.
- **Meta Ads:** Reels/Stories placements; separate wellness, creator and existing-customer audiences.
- **Google App Campaigns:** start only after both store listings and install measurement are live.
- **Owned channels:** Buffer/Instagram/TikTok/YouTube organic posts, with UTMs and RSID preserved.

## Measurement contract

Track and validate: ad click → store visit → install → first open → registration → email confirmation → onboarding completion → first chat → discovery action → qualified lead → paid conversion.

Use campaign-level UTMs and persistent RSID. Do not place email addresses, phone numbers, auth tokens, health details or other sensitive data in URLs or analytics payloads. Respect applicable platform consent requirements and privacy disclosures.

## Launch controls

- No spend before public store availability and end-to-end attribution QA.
- Start with capped experimental budgets approved by the owner.
- Optimise for activation and paid conversion, not cheap installs alone.
- Pause any creative whose claims no longer match the live app.
- Report spend, CPI, registration rate, activation rate, cost per qualified lead and CAC.
- No campaign has been created or activated by this document.
